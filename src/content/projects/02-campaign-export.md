A phishing campaign produces a lot of events. What a security officer needs from
it is one document they can forward to management without editing it first.

I led that summary export end to end: scoping with product, designing the
aggregation, building the rendering pipeline, and taking it through release.
Deciding what a reader who was never in the tool needs to see is most of the
work. The PDF is the easy half.
