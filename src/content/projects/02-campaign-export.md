Customers running phishing simulations needed to share results outside the
platform, and were rebuilding them by hand in spreadsheets.

I led this one end to end: scoping with product, designing the aggregation,
building the rendering pipeline, and taking it through release. The result turns
a campaign's raw event data into a summary document a security officer can send
to management without editing it first.
